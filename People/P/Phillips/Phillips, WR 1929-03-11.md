## 📖 Biography

**WR Phillips** was born 19 Mar 1929 in South Carolina and passed away leaving a distinguished legacy of service in the United States Air Force. 

During his career as a Master Sergeant, WR specialized in the establishment, construction, and maintenance of critical Cold War radar installations (such as the 7630th Radar Squadron in Lockport, NY, and the 877th ACWRON radar station perched atop Mount Gorramendi in Elizondo, Spain, under JUSMG-MAAG). While stationed on active duty in the USAF, he met his wife, Shirley Ann Whalen, who was also serving in the Air Force from 1954 to 1956.
