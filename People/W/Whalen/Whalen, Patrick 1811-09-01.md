# 👤 Patrick Whalen (1816–1893)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Great-great-grandfather of applicant [[Phillips, Lisa Michelle 1967-10-12|Lisa Michelle Phillips]]. Born in **Steuben, Washington County, Maine** (~1816) to Irish immigrant blacksmith **Thomas Whalen** and Passamaquoddy / Deer Island native **Joanna Flynn**. Married **Eliza Leslie** on November 26, 1840 in Charlotte County, New Brunswick. Lived and worked as a **commercial fisherman on Deer Island, West Isles Parish, Charlotte County, New Brunswick** for over two decades.
