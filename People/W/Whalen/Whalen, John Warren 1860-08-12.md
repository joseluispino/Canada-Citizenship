# ⚓ Capt. John Warren Whalen (1860–1936)

## 📌 Executive Summary
**Capt. John Warren Whalen** (August 12, 1860 – June 9, 1936) is the **Canadian-Born Root Ancestor (Generation G3) in Chain A**. Born on Deer Island, West Isles Parish, Charlotte County, New Brunswick, British North America, to [[Whalen, Patrick 1811-09-01\|Patrick Whalen]] (fisherman/sailor) and [[Leslie, Eliza 1824\|Eliza Leslie]]. Master boatbuilder, champion marksman, grocery merchant, and Eastport Deputy Sheriff.

Across a **30-year decennial census window (1900, 1910, 1920, 1930)**, Capt. Whalen was continuously sworn as an **Alien (`Al`)**, establishing that he never voluntarily naturalized in the United States and maintained uninterrupted British Subject / Canadian status.
