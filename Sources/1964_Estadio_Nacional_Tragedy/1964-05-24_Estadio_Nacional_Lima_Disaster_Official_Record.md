# 🏟️ Official Historical Dossier: The May 24, 1964 Estadio Nacional Tragedy

* **Event:** Olympic Football Qualifying Match: Peru vs. Argentina (Tokyo 1964 Tournament)
* **Date & Time:** Sunday, May 24, 1964, ~17:15 PET
* **Location:** Estadio Nacional del Perú, Calle José Díaz s/n, Lima, Peru
* **Attendance:** 53,000 spectators (Capacity Sold Out)
* **Casualties:** 328 officially recorded fatalities; over 500 critically injured. Recognized as the deadliest disaster in the history of association football.

---

### ⏱️ Chronology of the Disaster
1. **The Disallowed Goal (85th Minute):** With Argentina leading 1–0, Peruvian forward Kiki Lobatón scored a contested equalizer. Uruguayan referee Ángel Eduardo Pazos disallowed the goal for a foul.
2. **The Pitch Incursion & Police Violence:** Spectator Víctor Vásquez ("El Negro Bomba") and another fan stormed the pitch in protest. The Peruvian Republican Guard tackled them on the field, beating them with batons and releasing attack dogs in view of 53,000 spectators.
3. **The Tear Gas in the Grandstands:** Police Commander Jorge Azambuja ordered the firing of multiple tear gas canisters directly into the packed covered stands (*Tribuna Norte* and *Oriente*).
4. **The Crush at Puerta 10:** Thousands rushed down the enclosed concrete stairwells toward the exits. The heavy steel gates—specifically **Gate 10 (*Puerta 10*)**—had been locked shut from the outside with padlocks.
5. **The Fatal Crush & Looting:** The downward momentum crushed and suffocated hundreds against the solid steel doors. In the darkened aftermath outside the gates and stairwells, widespread looting broke out; thieves stripped watches and jewelry from the fallen, even cutting off fingers of victims to steal rings.

---

### 🖤 Paternal Family Loss & Morgue Identification
Attending the match that Sunday were paternal grandmother **[[Torres Romani, Maria Esther - URN-GEN-G0-METR|María Esther Torres Romani]]**, her son **[[Pino-y-Torres, Jose Andres - URN-GEN-G0-JAP|José Andrés Pino Torres]]**, and her brother.

Caught in the crush at Puerta 10:
* **María Esther Torres Romani** was tragically killed by asphyxiation/crush.
* **José Andrés Pino Torres** was tragically killed alongside his mother.
* The uncle who attended with them survived the crush, but had his finger severed by looters in the melee.
* **[[Torres Romani, [Paternal Great-Uncle] - URN-GEN-G1-PGU-TOR|Oscar's Father (Paternal Great-Uncle Torres Romani)]]** went to the central morgue in Lima to officially identify the bodies of his sister María Esther and nephew José Andrés.
