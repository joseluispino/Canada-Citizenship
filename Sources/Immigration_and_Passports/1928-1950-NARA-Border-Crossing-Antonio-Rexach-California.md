# 🇺🇸 US NARA Border Crossings & California Manifests: Comandante Antonio Rexach (1928–1950)

## 📌 Archival Metadata
* **Archive Repository:** National Archives and Records Administration (NARA, College Park, MD / San Bruno, CA)
* **Record Group:** Record Group 85 (Records of the Immigration and Naturalization Service)
* **Subject:** [[Rexach, Antonio 1900-1955 - URN-GEN-1900-AR|Comandante Antonio Rexach y Fernández de la Parga]]
* **Spouse Documented:** Katherine Randolph (1902–1985)
* **Key Locations:** Los Angeles, CA; San Diego / San Ysidro Port of Entry; La Paz, Baja California

## 📄 Archival Overview & Findings
1. **1928 California Marriage & Entry:** Documents Antonio Rexach, Spanish military aviator born in Burgos on September 23, 1900, entering the United States via California and marrying American citizen **Katherine Randolph** in Los Angeles in 1928.
2. **Cross-Border Commercial Operations:** Manifests record frequent transit between Southern California and Baja California in connection with *La Ahumadora de La Paz* and Pacific fisheries concessions.
3. **Immigration Status:** Alien Registration and non-resident visitor entries following his departure from Republican Spain in late 1936.
