# 🇺🇸 US Visa & Exchange Residence Registry: María Luisa Gutiérrez Rexach (1972–1980)

## 📌 Archival Metadata
* **Archive Repository:** National Archives and Records Administration (NARA, College Park, MD)
* **Record Group:** Record Group 59 (General Records of the Department of State) & RG 85 (INS)
* **Subject:** [[Rexach, Maria Luisa 1945-09-06 - URN-GEN-1945-09-MLR|María Luisa Gutiérrez Rexach]]
* **Spouse Documented:** [[Pino-y-Torres, Jose Luis 1944-12-14 - URN-GEN-1944-12-JLPT|Dr. José Luis Pino-y-Torres]]
* **Children Documented:** [[Pino, Jose Luis 1968-06-18 - URN-GEN-1968-06-JLP|José Luis Pino]], Andrés Pino, María Luisa Pino

## 📄 Archival Overview & Findings
1. **Transatlantic Family Arrival:** Documents the entry of María Luisa Gutiérrez Rexach (born September 6, 1945 in Madrid, Spain) accompanying her husband Dr. José Luis Pino-y-Torres to Minneapolis, Minnesota and later Maryland.
2. **Declared Lineage & Education:** Notes her Complutense University degree in Journalism and confirms parentage under **Dr. José Luis Gutiérrez de Allés** and **María Luisa Rexach Morales**.
3. **US Residence Period:** Documents her active family life in the United States prior to her eventual return to Madrid.
