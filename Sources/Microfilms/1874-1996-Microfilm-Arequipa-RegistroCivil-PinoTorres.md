* **Online Catalog URL:** [FamilySearch Catalog — Arequipa Registro Civil 1874-1996](https://www.familysearch.org/search/catalog/1410884)
* **Jurisdictional Coverage:** Municipalidad Provincial de Arequipa, Yanahuara, Cayma, Paucarpata, Tiabaya.

## 📄 Archival Summary & Research Scope
Handwritten annual civil registration folios for the urban center of Arequipa and Yanahuara district. The series contains birth (*nacimientos*), marriage (*matrimonios*), and death (*defunciones*) acts that establish the primary vital records for the **Pino**, **Torres**, and **Romani** paternal lineage branches.
