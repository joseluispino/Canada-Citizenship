   - *Previous Searched (Debunked Assumption):* Ancestry.com civil vital stats pre-1888, Drouin Roman Catholic collection, and Catholic parish catalogs (searched under the flawed heuristic that Irish ancestry = Catholic).

   - *Verified In-Vault:* Cataloged in PANB Series RS141B7 (Register B, Page 92) — Charlotte County Marriages.

## 1. Lineage Chain A: Whalen Lineage Search Queries

### A. Target: John Warren Whalen (b. August 12, 1860, New Brunswick)

> [!NOTE]
> Civil vital registration in New Brunswick did not begin until 1888. His official proof must come from **Catholic Parish Baptismal Records** or the **1861 Census of Canada**.

#### 🔍 Ancestry.com Search Parameters (Exact Values to Enter):
- **First & Middle Name**: `John Warren`
- **Last Name**: `Whalen` *(also check `Whelan` and `Whalin`)*
- **Event**: `Birth` → Year: `1860` $\pm 1$ year
- **Location**: `New Brunswick, Canada`
- **Father**: `Patrick Whalen`
- **Mother**: `Eliza Leslie`
- **Ancestry Collection Filter**: Select `Acadia, Canada, Vital and Church Records (Drouin Collection), 1757-1946` OR `Canada, Roman Catholic Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1760-1923`.

#### 🔍 FamilySearch Catalog Path (Free Unindexed Microfilms):
1. Go to [FamilySearch Catalog](https://www.familysearch.org/search/catalog)
2. Place: `Canada, New Brunswick, Charlotte` OR `Canada, New Brunswick, St. Andrews`
3. Category: `Church Records`
4. Target Collection: **Catholic Church. St. Andrew (St. Andrews, New Brunswick)** / **St. Croix Parish (St. Stephen, NB)**.
